# SIVA Techno Mart — Premium AI Academy & Entrepreneurship Hub

Act as a senior UI/UX designer, brand strategist, React architect, and frontend developer.

Build a premium, production-oriented website for:

**SIVA Techno Mart**

SIVA = Smart Intelligent Virtual Agent

**Primary positioning:** AI Academy & Entrepreneurship Hub

**Tagline:** Raising Your Skills to the Next Level with Superintelligent Tools

**Core message:** From AI Learner to Technology Creator. From Technology Creator to Entrepreneur.

## Business Objective

Create a professional AI technology education platform that helps students, working professionals, career changers, small business owners, and nonprogrammers learn practical AI skills, develop useful applications, and explore entrepreneurship.

The site should feel like a combination of a premium AI innovation company, modern engineering research lab, and professional technology academy.

## Brand and Visual Design

Use:
- Midnight navy #08162E
- Intelligence blue #1478D4
- Electric cyan #43D9EE
- Innovation gold #D9B86C
- Cloud white #F5F8FC

Design a cinematic, responsive homepage with sophisticated engineering, financial technology, and scientific visual themes.

Use abstract neural networks, elegant circuit graphics, mathematical patterns, data visualization, and modern technology imagery.

Avoid clutter, generic templates, exaggerated claims, and excessive animations.

## Academy

Provide course detail templates with:

- Course overview
- Intended audience
- Prerequisites
- Learning outcomes
- Proposed curriculum
- Delivery format
- Instructor information placeholder
- Enrollment inquiry CTA

Do not invent course dates, prices, certifications, or instructor credentials.

## Entrepreneur Launchpad

Create an engaging page that explains the journey from identifying a problem to launching an MVP.

Add a working idea-submission inquiry form. It should accept a visitor's idea, contact details, and type of assistance requested.

Do not automatically submit user ideas to third-party AI services.

## Technology

Use React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and accessible reusable components.

Implement:
- Responsive navigation
- Mobile-friendly layouts
- SEO metadata
- Accessible form validation
- Fast loading and optimized images
- Reusable page sections
- Configurable product and course content
- Clear privacy and terms placeholders
- A functional contact form with a secure backend or a clearly identified integration requirement

Do not simulate successful submissions when no backend is connected.

## Cost and Security

This is Phase 1 of the project.

Do not add paid AI APIs, chatbots, payment gateways, or user authentication unless essential.

Do not expose API keys in frontend code.

Keep dependencies minimal.

Do not create fake testimonials, customer counts, certifications, or business partnerships.
